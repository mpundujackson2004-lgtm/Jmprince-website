Welcome, friend

A ministry committed to sharing the Gospel

JACK MPUNDU PRINCE exists to encourage people through the Word of God, Christian preaching, Bible study, prayer and gospel music. Our desire is to point people to Jesus Christ and support spiritual growth in homes, churches and communities.

Explore the resources on this website and use them for personal devotion, group Bible study and ministry encouragement.

Our mission

To proclaim the good news of Jesus Christ, teach the Scriptures faithfully, encourage prayer, and serve others with love and humility.
